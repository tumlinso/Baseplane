CUDA remains optional. `Baseplane::seq` is the public exact library; the new workshop is an independently built experimental tree.

The [moonshot library](../../experiments/moonshot/README.md) now contains twelve family directories for automata, effects, hierarchy, rendezvous, tensor, demand, logic, incremental reuse, compiler, hypotheses, ports and machine experiments. These are concrete bounded sequence-grounded probes, not a general learned genome encoder.

The [normal moonshot build entry](../../experiments/moonshot/CMakeLists.txt) includes the family probes and [five executable compositions](../../experiments/moonshot/compositions/README.md): sequence effects, variant worlds, nonlocal context, compiled learning and ports/factors. ## Historical CUDA lab boundary

BitLift, CarryFold, Rethread and Rendezvous remain isolated experiments with their original results and evidence in the [CUDA lab](../../experiments/cuda_lab/README.md). The selection retained the restricted ordered affine CarryFold representation, with CUB as the faster measured executor. Rethread did not win its large fixture; BitLift loses arrangement/magnitude information; Rendezvous missed cross-warp peers and used oracle-assembled packets. The workshop does not turn those historical negative findings into new successes.

## Experimental limits and remaining scope

The workshop includes small synthetic learners and source-to-query compositions, with exact validity/provenance checks and explicit loss/refinement paths where implemented. A successful fixture or retained source pointer does not establish a lossless learned representation, biological entities or organism-scale utility. Family receipts distinguish authored, compiled, device-run and compared paths; the final normal-entry receipt is host evidence only and launches no GPU.

Five additional compositions remain explicitly deferred in the composition inventory: residual-backed latent context, dense possible-state distillation, blind-spot repair, budgeted precision and role-sketch relation retrieval. A trained genome-wide hierarchical embedding, broader online context construction, production buffer/runtime interfaces, workload-scale measurements and scientific nonlocal representation remain future work. The [later qualification plan](../../planning/baseplane_moonshot_bootstrap/docs/06_LATER_QUALIFICATION.md) retains the relevant obligations from the superseded deferred BitOp execution schedule. Completed historical evidence is preserved.

All old CUDA lab weights and routing keys were untrained. Its device-resident timings exclude allocation and transfer, and some information probes are scalar fixtures. The workshop's separate small synthetic fitting tests do not change that historical qualification. No new timing or biological efficacy claim follows from the host test pass.
